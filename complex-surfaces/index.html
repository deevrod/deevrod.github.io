Approximate schedule

  • Oct 1: initial session, distribution of topics
  • Oct 8: ??
  • Oct 12: motivation and examples. Two definitions of a complex manifold, Newlander–Nirenberg theorem. Parallels with and deviations from the classification of curves. Kodaira dimension (Rodion)
  • Vector bundles, line bundles, divisors. Statement of Serre's finiteness theorem. Mapping with a line bundle (Peters, Ch. 1)
  • Sheaves, coherent sheaves, sheaf cohomology. Dolbeault resolution, comparison theorem for Dolbeault and Čech cohomology
  • Stein manifolds, domains of holomorphy, Cartan's theorems A and B
  • Montel lemma, Cartan–Serre finiteness theorem. Definition of Kodaira dimension
  • Serre duality. Comparison with Hodge theory (without proof): Frölicher spectral sequence, Hodge decomposition
  • Chern classes, Noether formula, Riemann–Roch–Hirzebruch theorem
  • Intersection theory. Picard group, Albanese map, Nakai–Moishezon criterion. Birational invariance of Kodaira dimension
  • Positivity of Chern curvature vs. ampleness, Kodaira vanishing. Kodaira embedding theorem, Chow lemma, GAGA principle (without proof)
  • Blowing up, Castelnuovo's contraction theorem. Classification of rational and ruled surfaces. (Algebraic-geometric examples)
  • Elliptic surfaces, logarithmic transforms, (Tate—Shafarevich twists)
  • Castelnuovo's rationality criterion. Classification of projective surfaces
  • Torelli theorem for K3 surfaces. Principally polarised abelian surfaces are Jacobians, or products
  • Currents, Buchdahl–Lamari theorem. Classification of non-Kähler surfaces. Global Spherical Shell conjecture
  • (Surfaces of general type: Bogomolov–Miyaoka–Yau theorem, ball quotients, fake projective planes, Fano surfaces of lines)
  • (Differential geometry of complex surfaces: Seiberg–Witten monopoles as effective divisors (after Okonek–Teleman), Kobayashi–Hitchin correspondence over non-Kähler surfaces (after Buchdahl), Teleman's classification of surfaces of type VII with $b_2=1$)
